You want your site performing as well as it possibly can. So do we, and we would rather prove that than say it.

Every build is measured on Google’s Lighthouse, the tool Google itself scores a page with, and every page on this site comes back at 100 for performance.

It matters because nobody waits for a slow site. They go back and click the next one, and the customer you lost is the one you never hear from.
