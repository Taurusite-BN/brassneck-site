Live — first large project

  • WhoTrauma release therapy and breathwork coaching, based in the Netherlands. Works with individuals and with teams inside organisations.
  • What it neededTo be found and read in two languages, to get people as far as booking a first session, and to carry the disclaimers a health practice has to carry without those pages feeling like a warning.
  • What I builtA hand-written static site, no CMS to be taught. Every page in Dutch and English, a booking route, and the legal set — terms, medical disclaimer, privacy. Umami for analytics.
