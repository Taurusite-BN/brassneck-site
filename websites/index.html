Keeping it alive

Our sites are hand written and static. There is no WordPress, so there are no plugins, no security treadmill, and nothing that falls over at 3am because something updated itself while you slept. These plans keep the site current, not upright.

Lights On £20/mo Hosting, domain renewal, SSL, uptime monitoring, off-site backups, and a yearly check that every link and form still does what it says. Free for your first twelve months.
Care £60/mo Everything above, plus 45 minutes of content changes a month (unused time rolls over once), a monthly note on your traffic, and a reply inside two working days.
Partner £100/mo Everything above, plus an hour and a half of changes a month, a review call every quarter, and first claim on the diary when you want something new.

What is not in the price: photography, licensed fonts, paid software, and anything with the word “portal” in it. Those get named and costed before they happen, never afterwards.

How it goes

Six steps, and you are told which one you are on every time. There is no stage where the site disappears for a fortnight and comes back finished. What we need from you is in the second column, because the thing that actually delays a build is almost never the building.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    Half an hour, on the phone or in person, about what the site is for and who has to act on it. It sometimes ends with us saying you do not need one, or that the thing you actually need is a booking app somebody else already sells for a tenner a month.

    From you: nothing. Not even a brief.

    Half an hour
  2. 02

    Planning and design

    Structure first: what pages exist, what each one is for, and what you want somebody to do at the end of it. Then the look. Both are agreed before a line of code is written, because moving a page is cheap at this stage and expensive later.

    From you: your logo and any photography you own, and an hour to look at the plan properly.

    Week one
  3. 03

    Development

    Built by hand on a private link you can open any time and watch fill up. No staging password to remember, no screenshots emailed over, and no surprise at the end.

    From you: your words, or an hour of talking while we write them for you.

    Weeks one to four
  4. 04

    Revisions and feedback

    Two full rounds. You go through it on your own phone, in your own hands, and try to break it before anybody else gets the chance. Small changes after launch are not charged for.

    From you: one list rather than six emails, if you can manage it.

    Allow a week
  5. 05

    Launch

    Domain, SSL, analytics and the Google Business Profile link, all on from the first hour rather than switched on later when somebody asks how it is doing. We point the domain; you do not need to touch anything.

    From you: your domain login, or permission for us to buy the domain for you.

    One afternoon
  6. 06

    Maintenance, security and reports

    Hosting, domain renewal, SSL, backups and uptime monitoring, plus a plain monthly page on who came, what they did, and the one thing we would change next. The first twelve months of hosting are included in the build.

    From you: a reply when the report asks you a question.

    Ongoing, from £20/mo
