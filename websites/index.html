Keeping it alive

Our sites are hand written and static. There is no WordPress, so there are no plugins, no security treadmill, and nothing that falls over at 3am because something updated itself while you slept. These plans keep the site current, not upright.

Lights On £20/mo Hosting, domain renewal, SSL, uptime monitoring, off-site backups, and a yearly check that every link and form still does what it says. Free for your first twelve months.
Care £60/mo Everything above, plus 45 minutes of content changes a month (unused time rolls over once), a monthly note on your traffic, and a reply inside two working days.
Partner £100/mo Everything above, plus an hour and a half of changes a month, a review call every quarter, and first claim on the diary when you want something new.

What is not in the price: photography, licensed fonts, paid software, and anything with the word “portal” in it. Those get named and costed before they happen, never afterwards.
