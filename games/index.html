No trailer Sixty-three per cent of this game is currently a grey rectangle with a label on it, and the rest is placeholder. There will be footage when there is something worth pointing a camera at.

Every kid in this school has one.

Palm-sized plastic on wheels, tracks or legs, driven with a pistol-grip handset.

They fight them in bin lids, sinks, taped squares on the floor and the drain hollow behind the sports hall. There is an unwritten rulebook enforced by sixth-formers. There is a black market. There is a boy who will trade you a Series Two for whatever is in your pockets and is visibly not to be trusted.

The toys are the only things in the building with any colour in them.

Underneath the craze, there is something the school has quietly agreed not to talk about.
