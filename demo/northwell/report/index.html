The one thing we would change next

Put the phone number back on screen when people scroll.

The header number was tapped more than twice as often as the one at the bottom, and seven in ten visits are on a phone where the header scrolls away after one swipe. A call button that stays put on mobile is about half an hour's work and it is the only change this month that we think is worth making. Say the word and it is done before the weekend.
