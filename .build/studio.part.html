A studio that does both, on purpose.

A studio that builds websites and games sounds like a studio that does neither properly. It is a fair thing to wonder before you hand anyone money, so here is the honest answer rather than a slogan.

Brassneck

noun, British, informal. The nerve to do the thing you have not been given permission to do. Usually said with a degree of admiration.

We had the skills to do both. So we had the audacity to do both.

The same skill, in two rooms.

Every discipline here does the same work on both sides of the studio. The nouns change. The craft does not.

Three people, one small room.

Founder and lead developer Oliver Builds the sites and the game. A booking flow and a dialogue tree are closer than they look: both are state machines a stranger has to be able to walk out of without help.
Graphic designer James Draws the marks and everything that carries one. A logo that has to survive a browser tab and a key art that has to survive a store thumbnail are the same problem at two sizes.
Lead writer and tester Kaj Writes the words, then tries to break them. Clients get copy that survives a stranger reading it. The game gets a script that survives somebody playing it wrong on purpose.
