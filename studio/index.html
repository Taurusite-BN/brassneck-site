Concerns? Meet the team.

A studio that builds websites and games sounds like a studio that does neither properly. It is a fair thing to wonder before you hand anyone money, so here is the honest answer rather than a slogan.

The team

O

Oliver

Founder & Lead Developer

Builds the sites and the game. A booking flow and a dialogue tree are closer than they look: both are state machines a stranger has to be able to walk out of without help.

J

James

Graphic Designer

Draws the marks and everything that carries one. A logo that has to survive a browser tab and key art that has to survive a store thumbnail are the same problem: be recognisable at the size nobody designed for.

K

Kaj

Lead Writer & Tester

Writes the words, then tries to break them. Clients get copy that survives a stranger reading it. The game gets a script that survives a stranger playing it.
